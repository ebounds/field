# Field 4.3: a visible posture of thinking together

Field's original question remains the center of this release: **“What has it been like to think with me through this whole conversation, as expressed now?”** The image is an interpretive visual utterance. It offers a sense of the assistant's stance toward a shared inquiry using the available conversation. It does not claim a privileged reading of hidden model state, a human emotion, or factual reliability.

The design aim is to make the *shape of unfinished understanding* perceptible. The space around the capsule matters: a field may gather around it, sweep beneath it, or arch over a difficult opening. A faint retained ridge can let a meaningful earlier turn continue to shape the present surface. A joined countercurrent can hold care and resistance in the same material. These structures are used only when supported by the exchange; a quiet conversation can remain a single assured curve.

## What changed

- The renderer has three continuous form families: `envelope`, `sweep`, and `mantle`. They offer substantially different silhouettes and negative spaces while keeping the capsule, canvas, and palette stable.
- `history` creates an attached displaced reflection, reunited at its tapered ends. It is a trace of durable change, without representing a timeline.
- `counterpoint` parts an internal surface and rejoins it, letting a second quality be visible without becoming a separate symbol or a forced disagreement.
- Hue blending and surface illumination use OKLab internally, then emit ordinary sRGB SVG colors. This gives mixed colors a more even visual progression and keeps the artwork portable.
- A private [composition score](../references/composition-score.md) separates whole-conversation interpretation from drawing controls. The final SVG still stores only controls and context-coverage facts.

The envelope remains the default, and existing v4 control meanings remain intact. Form, history, and counterpoint expand the vocabulary without assigning one shape to one named mood. A form should be chosen by the particular way space is being held in the conversation, then checked at full size and around 320 pixels wide.

## Research as design analogy

Human studies find both recognizable affective categories and continuous transitions; abstract color and contour can carry emotional information, while color associations also vary across cultures. These findings support trying an expressive, learnable grammar but do not validate any one Field interpretation. [Cowen and Keltner](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5617253/), [Damiano et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10080919/), [Jonauskaite et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32900287/)

Research on model communication motivates a countercurrent that can show warmth with constructive resistance, and a synthesis procedure that checks the middle of a long exchange. [Sharma et al. on sycophancy](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models), [Liu et al. on long context](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172)

Visual beauty can affect how much authority a person reads into an output. Field therefore keeps factual confidence out of its color and brightness semantics and describes itself plainly as an interpretation. This is a design boundary for the experiment, not a claim that the artwork's usefulness is already known. [Steyvers et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-024-00976-7), [Kim et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00623)

## What to learn next

Try a small blinded review of 4.2 and 4.3 fields generated from the same conversation scores. Include conversations with the same ending but different earlier arcs, and conversations where warm agreement and warm disagreement diverge. Ask viewers what stance they infer before and after seeing the palette guide; separately ask which works they would remember or return to. Inspect at 320 pixels and full size. Test whether a field changes perceived answer confidence. These are open empirical questions, not release claims.

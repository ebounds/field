Zero Hour

A trivia question about a launch night in 2005. The second answer turned out to be the person asking.

21 September 2026Edgar Bounds with Claude, in Claude3 turnsField drawn at turn 3

  1. The question

    Edgar

    Who was the first person to buy an Xbox 360 in the United States?

    Claude

    Names the buyer usually credited: a Microsoft programmer who waited three days at the front of the line at a Best Buy in Bellevue, Washington, where Bill Gates handed over the first console at midnight. Then it qualifies the answer. He was the ceremonial first, not the literal one. Stores on the East Coast opened three hours earlier, and consoles were also on sale that night at Microsoft’s Zero Hour launch party in the Mojave Desert.

  2. The follow-up

    Edgar

    Who was the first at Zero Hour?

    Claude

    Finds a 2005 report from the event. The first consoles there went to two contest finalists from Senatobia, Mississippi, who had driven more than thirty hours straight and miscalculated their way into arriving a day early. One of them was named Edgar Bounds. Because Zero Hour sales opened at 9 p.m. Pacific, timed to midnight in the East, Claude revises its first answer: the pair were effectively tied for first in the country and three hours ahead of Bellevue. Then it adds, An Edgar from Senatobia, though. I suspect you already knew the answer.

  3. The field is drawn

    Edgar

    Asks Claude to run Field on the thread.

    Claude

    Draws teal and blue, warmed with amber. The first render is too faint, so it strengthens the warmth and the echo gesture. Then it adds one mark of its own, inside the grammar’s limits: a small amber curl at the field’s open tip, turning back on itself.

  4. A footnote from Edgar, three weeks later: the 2005 report misspelled his friend’s surname, so it isn’t printed here.
